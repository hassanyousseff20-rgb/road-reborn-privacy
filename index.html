Road Reborn — Privacy Policy

Effective Date: September 30, 2026

Nostalgia Demoz ("we", "us", or "our") operates the mobile game Road Reborn (the "Game"). This Privacy Policy explains how information may be handled when you use the Game.

1. Information We Handle

Depending on the features you use, the Game and its third-party service providers may handle the following categories of information:

Google Play Games Services

The Game uses Google Play Games Services for supported Google Play Games functionality and player identity. Information processed by Google Play Games Services may include information associated with your Google Play Games account or player identity and information required to provide the service.

Leaderboard Information

The Game uses Talo for its online leaderboard functionality. Leaderboard-related information may include your in-game username, best score, ranking information, and the player identifier required for the leaderboard service.

Talo is used by Road Reborn for leaderboard functionality only. It is not used for cloud save or general player-data storage.

Local Game Data

The Game stores gameplay information locally on your device. This may include:

  • Game progress and completed roads/worlds
  • Owned and unlocked cars
  • Coins and gameplay progression
  • Best score and related game statistics
  • Username and profile information
  • Game settings and preferences

Advertising Information

The Game uses Google AdMob to display rewarded and interstitial advertisements. AdMob and related Google advertising services may process information such as advertising identifiers, device information, IP address, approximate location derived from IP address, advertising interactions, and technical information, as applicable to the service, device, region, consent, and user settings.

2. How Information Is Used

Information handled by the Game and its service providers may be used to:

  • Provide and operate the Game and its features.
  • Save and maintain gameplay progress.
  • Provide Google Play Games functionality.
  • Provide online leaderboard functionality.
  • Display and measure advertisements.
  • Prevent fraud, abuse, and unauthorized activity.
  • Maintain the security and reliability of the Game.
  • Diagnose technical problems and improve stability.
  • Respond to support and privacy requests.

We do not sell your personal information.

3. Third-Party Services

  • Google Play Games Services — used for supported Google Play Games functionality and player identity.
  • Google AdMob — used to provide and measure advertisements.
  • Talo — used for online leaderboard functionality only.

These third-party services may process information according to their own privacy policies and terms.

4. Advertising

Road Reborn uses Google AdMob for rewarded and interstitial advertisements. Advertising services may use device and advertising information to provide, measure, personalize, and protect advertising services, subject to applicable laws, consent requirements, and device or account settings.

5. Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect information handled by the Game and its services. No method of electronic storage or transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

6. Data Retention

Local gameplay data remains on the user's device until it is deleted, reset, or otherwise removed by the user or the operating system.

Leaderboard and service-related information may be retained by the applicable third-party service as necessary to provide the relevant service, maintain records, prevent abuse, comply with legal obligations, and resolve disputes. Third-party providers may have their own retention and deletion policies.
